🚀 About SkillForge

SkillForge is an AI-powered platform designed for the EV & Automotive Manufacturing Ecosystem. It helps analyze resumes, identify skill gaps, and prepare individuals for the future of electric mobility.

📊 Key Features

📄 Resume Analysis

Upload resumes and extract skills automatically.

📉 Gap Analysis

Identify missing skills required in EV domain.

⚡ EV Readiness

Measure readiness for electric vehicle industry.

📈 Analytics Dashboard

Visualize workforce insights with data analytics.

🎯 Mission & Vision

Mission: Bridge the skill gap between current workforce and EV industry needs.

Vision: Become a leading platform for EV workforce transformation.

🛠 Technology Stack

HTML5 CSS3 JavaScript AI/ML Data Analytics EV Domain
